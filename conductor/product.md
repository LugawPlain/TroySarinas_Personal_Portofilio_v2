# Initial Concept

## Target Users

This portfolio website is primarily aimed at:

*   **Potential employers and recruiters:** To showcase my capabilities and experience for job opportunities.
*   **Fellow developers and tech enthusiasts:** To share knowledge, projects, and connect within the developer community.

## Goals

My main goals for this portfolio website are:

*   **Showcase technical skills and projects:** To demonstrate my expertise through practical applications.
*   **Highlight professional experience and education:** To provide a comprehensive overview of my career and academic background.
*   **Enable direct contact and networking:** To facilitate connections with interested parties for collaborations or opportunities.

## Key Features

To achieve these goals, the portfolio website should emphasize:

*   **Interactive project showcases with live demos:** Allowing visitors to experience my work firsthand.
*   **Detailed descriptions of professional experience and achievements:** Providing context and impact for my roles.
*   **An easily accessible and downloadable resume:** Offering a quick and convenient way for visitors to review my qualifications.
*   **An interactive AI chat widget for engaging with visitors:** To provide a dynamic and personalized interaction experience.
